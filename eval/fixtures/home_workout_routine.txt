Home Workout Routine - Full Body Focus
This weekly home workout routine is designed to help you grow your glutes, slim down upper legs,
and improve arm and back structure using minimal equipment. You'll work with resistance bands,
light weights, a jump rope, and bodyweight exercises.
Equipment Needed:
- Four 1kg weights
- One 6kg weight
- Jump rope
- Hula hoop
- Resistance bands
Weekly Schedule:
Day 1 - Lower Body Strength Focus
- Goblet Squats (6kg weight) - 3 sets of 12
- Resistance Band Glute Bridges - 3 sets of 15
- Bulgarian Split Squats (1kg weights in each hand) - 3 sets of 10 per leg
- Standing Kickbacks with Band - 3 sets of 15 per leg
- Wall Sit - 3 rounds of 30-60 seconds
Day 2 - Core & Cardio Focus
- Jump Rope Intervals - 5 rounds of 1 min on / 30 sec rest
- Plank with Shoulder Taps - 3 sets of 30 seconds
- Leg Raises - 3 sets of 12
- Russian Twists (1kg weight) - 3 sets of 20 (10 per side)
- Bicycle Crunches - 3 sets of 20
Day 3 - Active Recovery or Hula Hoop Play
- 15-20 min continuous hula hooping
- Light stretching and recovery
Day 4 - Glutes & Upper Legs Blast
- Banded Side Walks - 3 sets of 15 steps per direction
- Step-ups (with weights if needed) - 3 sets of 10 per leg
- Deadlifts (6kg weight) - 3 sets of 12
- Donkey Kicks (band or 1kg ankle weight) - 3 sets of 15 per leg
- Wall Sit Pulses - 3 rounds of 20 pulses
Day 5 - Total Body Conditioning
- Jump Rope - 1 min
- Bodyweight Squats - 20 reps
- Push-ups - 10-15 reps
- Weighted Lunges - 10 per leg
- Mountain Climbers - 30 seconds
- Plank - 45 seconds
Day 6 - Core Strength & Flexibility
- Deadbugs - 3 sets of 10
- Weighted Sit-ups (1kg weight) - 3 sets of 15
- Side Plank - 30 sec per side x 2
- Hollow Body Hold - 3 sets of 20 seconds
- Yoga or full-body stretching
Day 7 - Rest or Light Activity
- Light walk or casual movement
- Optional: 10-15 min hula hoop or jump rope
